# Work in progress: facts removed for lack of a citable source

A science value is shown on the site only when it has a citation with an https URL, a locator and a retrieval date.
Values that could not be qualified were removed from the public data and are listed here, with the source that would
qualify each one. Re-read dates are 2026-11-06 unless stated.

## Removed in this pass

| Element | Facet | Claim as it was shown | Source that would qualify it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mo | medical-devices | Molybdenum-99 is the parent isotope from which technetium-99m, used in diagnostic imaging, is generated. The cited National Academies chapter does not state this relation. | A source that states the Mo-99 to Tc-99m parent-daughter relation, such as a government or peer-reviewed nuclear medicine reference, read and quoted. |
| Gd | medical-devices | Gadolinium chelates act as MRI contrast agents by T1 shortening. | The full text of the Biomolecules 2022 review (PMC9221011) if its T1 section is quoted, or another peer-reviewed source on T1 relaxation. The abstract-level read did not confirm it. |
| H | states | Hydrogen is a main constituent of stars. | A NASA, NOAA or peer-reviewed page on stellar composition, read and quoted. |
| He | states | Helium is a main constituent of stars. | As for H: a stellar composition source, read and quoted. |
| H, He | states | NASA describes the Sun as a ball of hydrogen and helium. | The NASA Sun facts page (https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/), read for this sentence and quoted. |
| Li | states | The page reports evidence for condensation, not a direct atom-number measurement. | The full text of Physical Review Letters 75, 1687 (the body, not the abstract), read for the measurement method. |
| Ne, Ar | states | Neon and argon are used in gas-discharge tubes (neon signs). | A government or peer-reviewed source that uses the term gas-discharge tube, read and quoted. The NIST page read does not use it. |
| O | states | Energetic particles excite oxygen atoms, which emit green and red light in auroras. | The NASA auroras page, read for the mechanism sentence, and quoted. Only the colour sentence was quoted. |

Eight statements were removed. Nothing else was removed for lack of a source.

## Open decision

The Core lens shows the uses lists for each element. These are hand-written, carry no citation, and are marked
unaudited. They are shown because the lens is kept as "uses only". If uses are treated as science facts that must be
qualified, each list item needs a source, and until then the Core lens would show nothing.
