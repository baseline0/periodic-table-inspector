Methodology

How this site decides what it can say about an element, a company or a dispute

Back to the table

This site is a work in progress. It is not investment advice, and nothing on it is a statement of who owns, holds or has rights to any mine.

1. Element facts

Atomic number, symbol, mass, group, period, state and abundance come from standard reference values. The periodic table layout is fixed by the element data file.

2. How an element is extracted

Each element lists one or more extraction methods. A method describes how the element is obtained, not which company does it:

  • Mined as a primary ore or concentrate: the element is a principal target of the operation.
  • Recovered during refining of another metal: the element is a byproduct of another metal's smelting or refining.
  • Rare-earth mineral, ion-adsorption clay, evaporite or brine, cryogenic air separation, hydrocarbon processing: the deposit or process type.
  • Artificially produced: made in a reactor or accelerator. These elements are not mined.

The methods are our classification of published descriptions of how each element is obtained. They are not taken from any company's disclosure. Each one is marked unverified until it cites a source.

3. How a company is linked to an operation

A company is linked to a mining operation only when a source names it as the operator, owner or rights holder of that operation. Each link must record:

  • the source URL, such as a regulatory filing, an annual report or a government register;
  • the date the source was retrieved;
  • the name as the source gives it, plus any aliases.

We do not infer ownership from a name that merely looks similar. Searching for a company's name in news or court text is not enough to link it to a mine.

4. How a legal dispute is linked

A case is attached to an operation only when its court record, docket or case page names that operation or its operator. The case status comes from the court record. When a status cannot be confirmed, the page shows "status unverified" rather than guessing.

A dispute on this site is a filing, not a finding. Filing a case does not mean any party is liable.

5. What counts as verified

A panel shows a Verified badge only when every entry in it has at least one citation with a URL and a retrieval date. Without citations, the panel shows Unverified.

6. What this release withholds

The company and producing-country lists are withheld from this public release. They are not yet sourced, and a wrong name next to a mining-rights claim can cause real harm. They will return one entry at a time, each with a citation.

The reverse lookup from element to operation to dispute has its data model in place, but no operations or disputes have been added yet. Until they are, no link from an element to a company or case should be read as a finding.

7. What this site does not do

  • It does not give prices, valuations, forecasts or rankings of companies or elements.
  • It does not say whether any company or dispute is good or bad.
  • It is not legal advice.

8. Corrections

If something here is wrong, report it on the issue tracker. Include the page, the claim and a source URL where you can. A correction changes the record only once the source has been checked.
